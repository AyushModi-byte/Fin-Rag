Coverage

A small, fixed subset: 22 annual reports from 10 companies. Questions about anything else get "I don't know" without a model call.

    How it works

    Retrieval first, generation second. The model never sees anything that was not retrieved from the filing.

    1. RouteFinds the company and fiscal year in your question and restricts the search to that filing.
    2. RetrieveCombines meaning-based and keyword search over the filing's pages and tables, and keeps the best 8 passages.
    3. AnswerA language model answers from those passages only, cites each page it used, or says it doesn't know.
    4. CheckEvery citation opens the exact passage, so you can verify the number against the source.

    Test numbers were measured with 5 passages per answer. The live setting (8 passages) was only evaluated on the dev set, which is also the set it was tuned on, so expect results closer to the test column.

    • Check the cited page. On the held-out test, more than half of the answers (17 of 29) were judged wrong or "I don't know". Treat an answer as a pointer to the right passage, not as a verified figure.
    • Small sample. 29 questions per column: one question moves a score by 0.03.
    • Model-judged scoring. The same model family answers and grades; no human review.
    • Multi-page questions. Ratios that need two statements often fail when only one page is retrieved.
    • Scope. Ten companies, 22 filings, text and tables only (no scanned pages or charts).
